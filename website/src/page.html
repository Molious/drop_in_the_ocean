Studio

Night Shift Interactive

We're not building games you escape into. We're building games that reach out into your world, and transform it.

Night Shift Interactive is an independent studio pioneering a new kind of play: augmented, atmospheric, alive. We take your reality — the real road under your wheels, the real city outside your windscreen, the real motion of your drive — and we turn it into fantasy. Your evening commute becomes a mission through deep space. Your quiet backstreet becomes a contested sector. The world doesn't disappear when you play. It ignites.

This is gaming built on genuinely new mechanics. Your car's real movement steers the experience. Smooth, careful driving becomes power, resource, and reward. The light of real day and real night bends the story around you. Nothing is simulated that the world can give us for free — we simply reveal the adventure that was hiding in plain sight.

Our universe is Last Mile Legends, one living world across three games:

Celestial Co-Pilot
An AI intelligence rides shotgun on your real drives, turning every street into a sector and every journey into a mystery that deepens the more you travel.
Red Alert
Your cockpit becomes a starfighter. Map your city as a battlefield and bank the calm in your driving into firepower, hands on the wheel the whole time, because the ship only answers a steady pilot.
Poseidon
The frontier plunges beneath the waves, into a glowing, bioluminescent deep. The surface was only the beginning.

Your reality, turned to fantasy. This is the night shift.

  • Atmosphere first

    Sound, light and pacing come before feature lists. If a game doesn't feel like somewhere, we keep working.

  • Depth over size

    We'd rather ship one tight loop you can master than a sprawling map you forget.

  • Respect the player

    Fair prices, no dark patterns, and saves that survive real life getting in the way.
